# Vehicle asset attribution

`cesium-milk-truck.glb` is the Cesium Milk Truck sample from the KhronosGroup
glTF Sample Models collection.

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/tree/main/2.0/CesiumMilkTruck
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- Credit: Cesium for Everything, © 2017 Cesium
- SHA-256: `2E7600185BBCFE771F0A69A82EBC70D214D75380F31D079891548538F8F5AA3A`

The model is used as an optional utility/pickup visual and as the GLB-backed
delivery/service-truck variant. The procedural vehicle fallbacks remain
available if the asset is unavailable.

`toy-car.glb` is the Toy Car sample from the same Khronos glTF Sample Models
collection.

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/tree/main/2.0/ToyCar
- License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0)
- Credit: Guido Odendahl for the initial car model; Eric Chadwick for material and scene edits
- SHA-256: `9631414DB26EDC9A4E6DCCA7BDEDAC03C3AA6BA7926B35C6A6125E7D235A5605`

The model is used as an optional sedan visual. The procedural sedan fallback
remains available if the asset is unavailable.

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
